## FULL DESCRIPTION (400–600 words)

ReviewShield is an AI-powered online reputation management platform built for local businesses — restaurants, clinics, spas, and any business that lives and dies by customer reviews.

**The problem we solve:**
Most local businesses have no system to monitor what customers are saying across review platforms. A single negative review left unanswered can damage trust and drive away new customers. At the same time, business owners rarely have time to craft thoughtful, professional responses to every review they receive.

**What ReviewShield does:**
ReviewShield automatically monitors your reviews across Google, Yelp, and Facebook. The moment a new review appears — especially a negative one — you receive an instant email alert so you can respond before it hurts your reputation.

More importantly, ReviewShield uses Claude AI (Anthropic) to generate professional, context-aware responses to every review automatically. You can edit the AI-generated response before publishing it, making the process fast without sacrificing authenticity.

**Key capabilities:**
- Real-time monitoring of Google, Yelp, and Facebook reviews
- Instant email alerts for negative reviews (1-3 stars)
- AI-generated response drafts ready to copy and post
- Editable AI responses — tweak before publishing
- On-demand AI response regeneration (up to 3x per review)
- Reputation analytics dashboard with rating trends
- Animated donut chart showing positive vs. negative breakdown
- PDF export of all reviews and AI responses (for reporting)
- White Label option for agencies managing multiple clients
- Multi-language support: English and Spanish (auto-detected)
- 14-day free trial, no credit card required

**Who it's for:**
ReviewShield is designed for local business owners who want to protect and grow their online reputation without spending hours monitoring review platforms. It's also ideal for marketing agencies managing reputation for multiple clients (Agency plan includes white label branding).

**Why ReviewShield:**
Unlike enterprise reputation tools that cost hundreds per month, ReviewShield is affordable for small and medium local businesses. Setup takes minutes — connect your business, select your platforms, and ReviewShield starts monitoring immediately.
